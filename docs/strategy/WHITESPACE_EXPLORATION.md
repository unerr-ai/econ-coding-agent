## The findings that matter (post-verification)

### 1. Defense is still the cleanest whitespace — and survived verification
Classified defense software runs on air-gapped LANs (Security Manual: biometric access, no
internet, no cloud) — US tools are *unusable*, not merely expensive. iDEX ₹498.78 cr (verified on
ddpmod.gov.in) has funded ~300 startups; DRDO's 100+ labs and 15 defense PSUs code manually. RDP
Technologies proved the air-gapped procurement path for GPU *infra*; the agent layer on it doesn't
exist. econ is pre-adapted: all-OSS tiers, self-hosted, no external APIs, governance gate as the
audit story. Friction (12–24 mo cycles, clearances) is the moat. (The ADITI ₹25 cr per-startup
grant ceiling was subsequently verified on ddpmod.gov.in — see the verification ledger below.)

### 2. "Sovereign agentic ops" is a twin of the defense finding, at commercial scale
The second sweep found the same *hard-blocked buyer* shape in civilian form: Indian banks/fintech
cannot adopt US AI-SRE agents (Resolve, Traversal, Cleric — silent on DPDP/RBI) or US-ingesting
observability, because RBI FREE-AI demands board-approved AI policy, model approval, human-in-loop
on high-impact actions, and cryptographic audit chains, while payment data must stay on-soil.
econ's loop transfers: cheap-tier triage → escalate root-cause (call-graph context is the
differentiator vs telemetry-only vendors) → governance gate on the *remediation action*. Two
entry tiers: ~200 regulated entities paying a compliance premium, and an untouched MSP/mid-market
layer. This is the strongest *new* direction from the second sweep.

### 3. The governance-gate product: EU urgency moved out 18 months; India is now the near-term driver
Verification corrected the first sweep's central date: EU AI Act high-risk obligations (incl.
Art-12 logging) slipped from Aug 2026 to **Dec 2027** (stand-alone) / Aug 2028 (embedded). The
"30-day window" framing was wrong. The gap itself is confirmed — observability vendors log
post-hoc, governance vendors write policy, nobody enforces at the action with code-anchored audit
trails — but the near-term buyers are Indian: DPDP (Nov 13 2026 consent framework, May 13 2027
full compliance — verified) and RBI FREE-AI. Sequence India-first, EU as the 2027 second act.
Same asset sells inward as the GCC platform layer (EY-verified 58% agentic-AI investment).

### 4. The India SMB developer long tail is unserved where the platforms can't see it
SAP/Salesforce/ServiceNow are closed by first-party agents whose lock-in is certification and
clean-core governance, not contracts. But below the platform vendors' sightline: **Tally (~5M
Indian SMBs, zero AI dev tooling, a BASIC dialect absent from training corpora)**, Zoho Creator
Z-code, Odoo custom modules. Cheap-tier economics make $5–20/mo viable against $5K+ consulting
projects. Small ACVs, huge count, India-native distribution — and nobody is there.

### 5. Export: Malaysia first, Indonesia demoted, Gulf closed
Malaysia's sovereign full-stack AI infra is verified (RM2B, launched May 2026, first in SEA), the
government channel is warm, and no local coding-agent vendor exists. Indonesia's premise reversed
on verification — GR 71/2019 *permits* private-sector foreign SaaS (public sector still localizes),
so the wedge there is government/SOE only. The Gulf ships its own agent platforms (HUMAIN, G42).
The competitive clock everywhere is Chinese vendors, who own model layers but not agent products.

### 6. Tax/CA: the cautionary tale for this whole exercise
The first sweep scored it OPEN on two claims that died under verification: "70K daily users" (real
figure ~30K members total, mid-2024, per ICAI itself) and "no agentic specialist exists" (Covoro/
GSTHero runs autonomous GST agents across 10K+ enterprises; June AI likewise; ICAI×Sarvam MoU of
June 26 2026 claims the sovereign institutional layer). Two lessons: (a) verify before betting;
(b) Sarvam is expanding from models into institution-distributed professional applications —
exactly the pivot risk [COMPETITORS.md](./COMPETITORS.md) flagged. Track which institute or
regulator Sarvam partners with next.

## Third sweep (2026-07-04): the whitespace is ONE category — the sovereign public sector

Six parallel sweeps over the never-touched axes (civilian gov, space, critical-infra OT,
EDA/embedded, pharma GxP, leftovers). The result is not a list of new fields — it's a
**unification**:

**1. The hard-blocked buyer replicates only inside government.** Defense (rows 1, and
[DEFENSE_OPPORTUNITY.md](./DEFENSE_OPPORTUNITY.md)), space/ISRO (row 21), civilian government
software factories (row 20), and — via the CEA 2025 isolation mandate — critical-infra PSUs
(row 22) are **the same buyer category**: government and government-adjacent establishments whose
networks are isolated by law or policy, who cannot buy US cloud AI tools at any price, and whose
funding arrives through grant/mission channels (iDEX/ADITI ₹750 cr, IN-SPACe TAF ₹500 cr,
IndiaAI ₹2,000 cr, RDSS). One product (on-prem/air-gapped agentic platform + governance gate +
audit trail), one BD motion, concentric rings by clearance friction: **defense core → space/
nuclear → civilian gov (NIC/C-DAC/CRIS) → regulated PSUs.** Sarvam is the common competitor on a
6–12 month clock in every ring (MoD discussions, ICAI, and now a Series B explicitly naming
agentic + coding).

**2. Outside government, the hard-block thesis degrades to compliance-evidence-as-a-feature.**
Pharma (Pfizer runs Copilot), automotive (Renault runs Gemini on Google Cloud), EDA (GCCs use
cloud) — none is blocked; all must *evidence* their AI-generated changes (GAMP 5, MISRA C:2025,
ISO 26262/ASPICE, IRDAI's coming framework). That's the governance gate sold as vertical
packaging — roadmap features for the commercial product, **not** new fields or tracks.

**3. Nothing in sweep 3 is a new independent field.** Row 20 (civilian gov) is the strongest
finding and it is an *extension ring* of the defense direction, not a separate bet. Rows 21–22
are beachheads/channels inside the same category. Rows 23–26 are feature plays or incumbent-owned.
Row 27 corrects the defense export plan (Gulf = JV-only).

Standing discipline applies: all sweep-3 rows are unverified. Before any bet-sized action on
row 20, verify: NICNET/MeitY policy on cloud AI tools inside gov networks, an actual procurement
pipeline (GeM/tenders), and real developer headcounts (the 3.5–12.5K range is too wide to plan on).
